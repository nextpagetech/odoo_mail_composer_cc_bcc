Odoo Mail Composer

Email CC and BCC

Email CC and BCC adds partner-based Cc and Bcc recipient fields to the Odoo mail composer. It lets users include additional visible and blind-copy recipients while sending emails from the composer, with optional company defaults and email template Bcc support.

Overview

This addon extends Odoo Mail with Cc and Bcc partner fields in the email compose wizard. It stores selected Cc and Bcc partners on mail messages, prepares outgoing mail with Cc and Bcc email headers, and adds Bcc support on email templates. Company-level default Cc and Bcc partners can be configured and prefilled in the composer.

Key Features

Composer Cc recipients

Adds a Cc partner field to the Odoo mail composer using the email tag widget.

Composer Bcc recipients

Adds a Bcc partner field for blind-copy recipients in composer email sending.

Company default recipients

Adds default Cc and Bcc partner fields on the company form and preloads them into new composer records.

Email template Bcc

Adds a Bcc field to email templates and applies matching template recipients when the composer uses a template.

Message and mail visibility

Stores Cc and Bcc partners on mail messages and shows Bcc on outgoing mail records.

Follower inclusion during sending

When sending from a record composer, current followers are reflected into the Cc flow used for notification delivery.

How It Works

  1. Configure optional default Cc and Bcc partners on the company record.
  2. Open the Odoo mail composer from a supported record.
  3. Select To, Cc, and Bcc recipients as partners using the email tag fields.
  4. If an email template is selected, matching template Cc/Bcc email addresses are resolved to partners where possible.
  5. Send the email. The addon prepares outgoing mail with Cc and Bcc values and stores the selected recipient information on the message.

Use Cases

  • Sales, support, or operations teams that need visible copy recipients on outgoing Odoo emails.
  • Managers or internal teams that need blind-copy visibility on selected customer communications.
  • Companies that want common Cc and Bcc partners prefilled from company defaults.
  • Email templates that require a Bcc recipient address during composer-based sending.

Configuration / Setup

No separate configuration menu is added. Optional default Cc and Bcc partners can be set on the company form. Email template Bcc can be configured on email templates where the template form is available.

CC and BCC in the Mail Composer

The composer adds partner-based Cc and Bcc fields below the standard recipient area, allowing users to select additional recipients before sending.

Defaults and Template Recipients

Company defaults can prefill Cc and Bcc partners, and email templates can provide Bcc recipient addresses that are applied when using the composer.

Business Benefits

  • Adds familiar Cc and Bcc recipient handling to Odoo email composition.
  • Reduces manual recipient entry by supporting company default partners.
  • Helps keep internal stakeholders copied on selected outgoing communications.
  • Stores Cc and Bcc recipient information on generated mail messages.

Known Scope

This addon works with partner-based Cc and Bcc fields in the standard mail composer flow. It does not target mass mailing mode.
