Odoo Mail

Email CC and BCC

Adds separate To, Cc, and Bcc recipient handling to the Odoo mail composer, with company defaults and email template support.

Overview

Email CC and BCC extends Odoo Mail so users can manage carbon copy and blind carbon copy recipients directly from the mail composer. It also adds company-level default Cc and Bcc partners, stores Cc and Bcc recipients on mail messages, and adds Bcc support on email templates.

Key Features

Cc and Bcc in composer

Adds partner-based Cc and Bcc fields to the mail compose wizard.

Company defaults

Adds default Cc and Bcc partner fields on the company form for composer defaults.

Email template support

Adds a Bcc field to email templates and uses template Cc/Bcc emails to find matching partners for the composer.

Mail record visibility

Adds Bcc to outgoing mail and stores Cc/Bcc partner recipients on related mail messages.

How It Works

  1. Configure default Cc and Bcc partners on the company form when needed.
  2. Open the Odoo mail composer from a document or record.
  3. The composer shows separate Cc and Bcc partner fields.
  4. Selecting an email template can populate Cc and Bcc partner fields from matching template email addresses.
  5. When sent from the composer, Odoo includes the Cc and Bcc recipients in the outgoing email handling.

Benefits

  • Separates direct recipients from Cc and Bcc recipients in the mail composer.
  • Reduces repeated entry of common Cc and Bcc recipients through company defaults.
  • Supports template-driven Cc and Bcc recipient selection when matching partners exist.
